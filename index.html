Word Order

Statements use SVO (Subject-Verb-Object). Questions use VSO.

Eo drala irhi — He speaks the language (SVO)
Drala eo irhi? — Does he speak the language? (VSO)

Negation

Particle na after the verb.

Ean dralan na — I don't speak

Articles

eo = definite (pre-nominal). -ri = indefinite (enclitic, after apostrophe).

eo ferag — the temple
ferag'ri — a temple

Possession

re + noun = analytic (pronouns, proper nouns). noun + 'es/'e = synthetic (common nouns).

irhi re Kaharin — language of Kaharin
ferag'es irhi — temple's language

After sibilants (/s/, /z/), use -e instead of -es.

tibilidis'e — scripture's (not *tibilidis'es)

Plural

Back vowels (/a,o,u/) → -al. Front vowels (/e,i/) → -el.

ferag'al — temples (ferag → /a/ → back)
irhi'el — languages (irhi → /i/ → front)

Verb Morphology

Six slots: ROOT + ASPECT + TENSE + MOOD + PERSON + na

dral-en-ashi-uk-an — I would have been speaking

Adpositions

Locatives attach after the noun with apostrophe: serak'zal "in the house". rit "with" is free-standing.

ferag'zal — in the temple
rit moimas tor — with a warm coat

Compound Verbs

Adposition + verb root, direct fusion, no apostrophe.

deorbrec- — remove (deor "from" + brec- "take")
ritbrin- — share (rit "with" + brin- "give")

Questions

WH-words: ego (what), egu (who), ege (where), ego'shi (when), ego'kes (why), ego'mesh (how).

Ego dralad? — What do you speak?
Egu eger? — Who are you? (lit. who are-you)
Ege horad? — Where do you go?
